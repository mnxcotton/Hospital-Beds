# Hospital-Beds
Examining hospital bed management with machine-learning methods. I will explore datasets containing information on hospital staffing and bedding operations and find insights on hospital bed management.

## Background: 
Many hospitals are often dealing with finding enough hospital beds for the flux of patients who are admitted into the institution. According to online articles from NPR and JAMA Network, many hospitals in the U.S. in recent years face hospital bed shortage. A recent example that magnified this issue is from the COVID-19 pandemic. 

## Description of Data Sets
The dataset is based on a simulation of medium-sized hospital. Contains information on patient admission, staffing, bed availability, and hospital services. The dataset consists of 4 different .csv files.

### patients.csv 
- Information about patients
- Arrival and departure dates from hospital admission, the services they requested during their time at the hospital, and their satisfaction review of specified service. 
- Contains 7 variables and 1000 observations.

### staff.csv
- List of hospital employees and the services they provide for patients.
- Contains 4 variables and 110 observations.

### staff_schedule.csv
- A year's worth of work schedules at the hospital. Shows which employees were present at the hospital during a particular week.
- Contains 6 variables and 6552 observations.

### services_weekly.csv
- Records of services provided to patients grouped by week and month. Lists the number of beds available, the number of patients who requested//refused/admitted to a hospital bed, special events that happened in the hospital, aggregated patient satisfaction, and the staff morale for each particular week/month.
- Contains 10 variables and 208 observations.
